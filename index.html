PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: December 2025

Welcome to Wonka Kids! We are committed to protecting your privacy and the privacy of your children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our educational app.

1. INTRODUCTION

Wonka Kids is an educational memory game designed for children ages 3-8. We understand the importance of protecting children's privacy, and we are committed to complying with applicable privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect minimal information necessary to provide and improve our app experience:

  • Game Progress Data: We store your child's game progress locally on your device, including:
    • Game scores and stars earned
    • Completed stages and unlocked content
    • Daily mission progress
    • Badges and achievements
    • Star points balance
    • Wonka card collection
  • Child's Name: You may optionally enter a child's name for personalization. This is stored locally on your device only.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic device information (device type, iOS version) for app functionality and troubleshooting.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • Track game progress and achievements
  • Personalize the app experience
  • Improve app performance and fix technical issues

We do NOT:

  • Sell or rent your information to third parties
  • Use information for advertising purposes
  • Share information with other apps or services
  • Collect location data
  • Collect contact information
  • Use information for marketing purposes

4. DATA STORAGE

All game data is stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults system. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • We cannot access your data remotely
  • If you delete the app, all data is removed
  • You can reset all data at any time through Settings

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

Wonka Kids is designed for children and complies with COPPA:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not require account registration
  • We do not collect email addresses or other contact information
  • We do not enable social sharing features
  • All data remains on the device and is not transmitted to external servers

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Core: We use Firebase for basic app functionality. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are configured but currently disabled. No personal data is transmitted to Firebase.
  • Apple Services: The app uses standard Apple services (UserDefaults, iCloud if enabled) which are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • Data is encrypted using iOS standard encryption
  • We do not transmit data over the internet
  • Regular iOS security updates protect your device

8. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All data is stored on your device and accessible through the app
  • Delete your data: Use the "Reset All Data" option in Settings to delete all information
  • Opt-out: You can stop using the app at any time and delete it from your device

9. PARENTAL CONTROLS

Parents and guardians can:

  • Review all data stored in the app through the Profile section
  • Reset all data at any time through Settings
  • Delete the app to remove all data
  • Control device-level privacy settings through iOS Settings
